About me

Get to Know Onome Precious.

I am Omojuwa Onome Precious, a biochemist based in Lagos State, Nigeria, with experience in a laboratory environment and in hospitality as a Guest Relations Officer at Five Clover Group of Hotels. My professional interests bring together scientific thinking, human relations, customer service, and the quality of everyday interactions.

Biochemistry encourages a careful, analytical approach to information and an appreciation for accuracy and responsibility. My laboratory background at the Centre for Forensic and DNA Studies Lab is part of that professional journey. In hospitality, guest relations places human connection, clear communication, and attentive service at the centre of the experience.

I value respectful communication, thoughtful problem-solving, and making people feel heard. I aim to bring these qualities to professional relationships and customer-facing environments.
